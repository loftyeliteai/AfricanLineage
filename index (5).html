A digital home for African ancestry, migration stories, and family memory

This project is built for families who want more than a pedigree chart. It brings together names, dates, records, photos, and oral history in one private research space.

Why it exists

Many African and diaspora families are reconstructing history across migration, colonization, name changes, and incomplete records. The platform is designed to support that reality instead of pretending every archive is complete.

Who it serves

Families, community historians, and diaspora researchers who want a simpler way to preserve lineage knowledge without losing context.

What makes it different

It balances research tools with storytelling. You can organize evidence while still keeping the human voice of the family intact.

Questions

Common things families ask before they begin

Straightforward answers on privacy, local testing, and mobile access.

Can I use the site before Supabase is connected?

Yes. Local mode lets you test the experience in one browser before connecting a live Supabase project.

What can I do in the MVP?

Create a tree, add relatives, upload family images, search records, and save likely matches for later research.

Is my family data private?

Yes. Private tree data is tied to your account, and the product is designed around family-controlled workspace data.

Can relatives use it on phones?

Yes. The layout is responsive and the core tree, gallery, and search views work on both desktop and mobile screens.
